---

# Philosophy

LCGC is not an AI classification system.

LCGC is an AI Comparator that finds the most visually similar laterite material from a validated comparator database.

Every design decision should prioritize:

- Speed
- Simplicity
- Professionalism
- Readability
- Field usability

---

# Design Principles

1. Mobile First
2. One Hand Operation
3. Maximum 2 Clicks to Scan
4. High Contrast
5. Scientific Appearance
6. Dark Industrial Theme
